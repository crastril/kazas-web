# Product Brief: Kazas

## Executive Summary

Kazas aims to redefine the seasonal rental market in Martinique by introducing a "St-Barth style" luxury standard to an ecosystem currently characterized by inconsistent quality. By offering a comprehensive, end-to-end management service—ranging from renovation and interior design to high-yield revenue management and white-glove concierge services—Kazas enables villa owners to maximize their asset's performance without the operational burden.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

The Martinique seasonal rental market suffers from a "quality ceiling." While there is high demand for luxury experiences, existing offers often fall short in terms of property standing and service quality. Villa owners possess valuable assets but lack the integrated expertise (renovation, marketing, hospitality) to unlock their full financial potential and reach the premium "St-Barth" standard.

### Problem Impact

-   **For Owners:** Suboptimal yield, lower occupancy rates, and asset stagnation.
-   **For Guests:** Disappointment due to the gap between price and actual service/quality.
-   **Marketwise:** Martinique loses high-end clientele to other Caribbean destinations with more mature luxury offers.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

Current local actors operate in silos (agencies do marketing *or* concierge *or* renovation) and often target the mid-range market. They lack the "premium DNA" required to elevate a property to a luxury asset class, resulting in average performance for potentially exceptional properties.

### Proposed Solution

Kazas provides a vertically integrated "Luxury Villa Management as a Service." We take a holistic approach:
1.  **Asset Elevation:** Renovation and design services to upgrade the property.
2.  **Strategic Positioning:** High-end "St-Barth style" branding and listing creation.
3.  **Operational Excellence:** Full concierge services, algorithmic price optimization, and guest experience management.

### Key Differentiators

-   **Full-Stack Expertise:** We handle everything from the bank dossier for funding to the daily guest welcome, unlike competitors who only manage bookings.
-   **Proven "Secret Sauce":** A reproducible, process-driven model for turning underperforming assets into high-yield luxury products.
-   **Caribbean Luxury Aesthetic:** A specific "St-Barth" brand identity that signals exclusivity and quality instantly.

-   **Caribbean Luxury Aesthetic:** A specific "St-Barth" brand identity that signals exclusivity and quality instantly.

## Target Users

### Primary Users: The Profit-Driven Owner
**Profile:** Owners of high-standing villas in Martinique, generally viewing their property as an investment asset.
**Mindset:** "Investor." They prioritize financial performance over emotional attachment.
**Key Pain Point:** Underperformance. They seek to maximize ROI and are frustrated by the low yield or "low quality" management of standard local agencies.
**Goals:** Maximize occupancy and nightly rates (Yield), maintain the asset's high standing (Renovation), and minimize effort.

### Secondary Users: The Francophone Luxury Traveler
**Profile:** Tourists primarily from France and Quebec seeking a seamless tropical luxury experience.
**Expectations:** A "St-Barth" standard of service and accommodation. They equate price with perfection and are sensitive to the quality of amenities and concierge services.
**Role:** Their satisfaction is the engine of the Owner's ROI.

### User Journey (Owner)
1.  **Discovery:** Realizes their property is under-monetized. Finds Kazas proposing a premium upgrade.
2.  **Audit (The Hook):** requests a free "Performance Audit" via the landing page.
3.  **Onboarding:** Receives a tailored quote (Work/Deco + Management).
4.  **Passive Income:** Kazas takes over operations. The owner checks performance reports.
5.  **Success:** Revenue increases, property value is maintained/enhanced.

5.  **Success:** Revenue increases, property value is maintained/enhanced.

## MVP Scope

### Core Features (The Launchpad)
-   **Brand Identity:** "St-Barth standard" web design (typography, animations, imagery) to justify premium positioning.
-   **Lead Capture Funnel:** "Request Audit" flows with specific fields (location, size) to pre-qualify leads.
-   **Portfolio Showcase:** Grid display of managed villas with "Success Story" angles (Yield stats, Renovation transformations).
-   **Service Pillars:** Dedicated content for Conciergerie, Renovation, and Revenue Management.
-   **Contact & Trust:** Prominent contact options (WhatsApp/Phone) and "Superhost" trust signals.

### Out of Scope for MVP
-   **Direct Booking Engine:** Bookings will be redirected to Airbnb/Booking.com links initially to simplify operations.
-   **Owner Portal:** No login area for owners. Reporting will be handled manually (PDFs via email) to start.
-   **Interactive Calendar:** Availability will not be real-time on the marketing site.

### MVP Success Criteria
-   **Technical:** fast load times (<2s) despite high-res imagery.
-   **Business:** 10 Qualified Leads generated via the "Audit" form in Month 1.
-   **Aesthetic:** Validation by user that the site feels "Luxurious" and "Premium".

### Future Vision
-   **Kazas Club:** Exclusive direct booking platform to bypass OTA fees.
-   **Owner App:** Real-time dashboard for yield and reservation tracking.
